My story

How I found my way to HR

I didn't start out in HR. I studied English for Industries at UNISEL, and my first roles were in administration: organising meetings and events, keeping records in order, and supporting the people who led the teams. Somewhere along the way I noticed that the part of the job I enjoyed most was the people side, whether that was helping a new staff member settle in or making sure someone got paid correctly and on time.

At the Youth Empowerment Foundation I helped more than 50 students get placed into public universities and technical colleges, and looked after the welfare of the students living in the Foundation's transit home. Seeing how much the right support can change someone's path stayed with me.

Since then my career has moved steadily towards HR. Today I handle the full spectrum, from payroll and statutory contributions to foreign worker compliance, recruitment, discipline and training, and I'm deepening that knowledge through a Master's in Human Resource Management.

"Good HR is simple: people should be treated fairly, paid correctly, and supported to do their best work."

That's the standard I hold myself to every day, and it's what I'd bring to your team.

About me

People first, details always.

I'm an HR and Administration professional with over six years of experience across telecommunications, property management and corporate services. My work covers the full HR spectrum: payroll and statutory contributions, foreign worker matters, recruitment and onboarding, employee and industrial relations, and training and development.
