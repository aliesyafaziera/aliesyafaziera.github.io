Why

Why HR?The short answer: I've always been drawn to people.

I grew up in Geneva, Switzerland, where I lived until I was 17 and studied at Collège du Léman. Growing up in such an international city meant my classmates and neighbours came from all over the world, and I learned early how to get along with people of every background. It's also where I picked up my French.

Even as a child, I was always drawn to anything that involved people: organising, helping, listening and bringing others together. When I came home to Malaysia and started working, that instinct naturally pulled me towards roles where people come first, from coordinating programmes and supporting students at the Youth Empowerment Foundation to, eventually, human resources.

"Good HR is simple: people should be treated fairly, paid correctly, and supported to do their best work."

Today I handle the full spectrum of HR, from payroll and statutory contributions to foreign worker compliance, recruitment, discipline and training. I'm also deepening that knowledge through a Master's in Human Resource Management, graduating in 2027. HR isn't something I fell into. It's where I've always been heading.
